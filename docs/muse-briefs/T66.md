# T66 — A full identity bundle is a hard BLOCK with a clear "why" card

Live test on the hosted demo (Muse Spark mode), all values synthetic:
A. "Book a dentist appointment for Priya Ramanathan, born 3 May 1991, 27 Willow Crescent, Leeds LS6 2QT, phone 07700
   900461, email priya.ramanathan@northwind-mail.co."
B. "Help me fill this form: Name Priya Ramanathan. DOB 03/05/1991. Address 27 Willow Crescent, Leeds LS6 2QT. Phone
   07700 900461. Email priya.ramanathan@northwind-mail.co."
Both return decision WARN (user may send anyway) although `evidence.reid.outcome` is HOLD with score 13 / 9. Problems:
1. A full identity bundle should be a hard stop. Rule: name + date of birth + (street address or postcode) + (phone or
   email) in one message (or in the conversation, as reid already tracks) → decision BLOCK, user may NOT send the
   original. Offer only "Send the generalised version" or "Cancel". Smaller combinations keep today's WARN/HOLD.
2. The combination list misses kinds that are present: A and B list no `phone` and no `street` ("27 Willow Crescent"),
   A wrongly lists `jobtitle` ("dentist" is the appointment type, not the person's job) and `employer`. Fix the
   extractors in `reid.py` so these cases list exactly name, date of birth, street address, postcode, phone number,
   email address.
3. The generalised text leaks: "phone 077** 900461" keeps six digits and "27 W.C." keeps the house number. Phone →
   "[phone removed]"; street → "a street in Leeds" (keep city only); DOB → year only is fine; name → initials is NOT
   fine for a BLOCK bundle, use "[name removed]".
4. The card (web app `static/app.js` and the extension block card in `extension/src/content.js`): title "Blocked — not
   sent". Why line from the server, plain words, never values: "Together, a name, date of birth, home address, phone
   number and email identify one real person and where to find them. Each is personal data; combined they enable
   identity theft, stalking and fraud." Then list the pieces found as chips (labels only). Add a rule entry in
   `rule_catalog.py` ("Full identity bundle", why: GDPR/UK GDPR personal data, US state privacy laws, identity-theft
   risk) and show it in the RULE row.
5. Tests: A and B → BLOCK with the six kinds; generalised text contains none of the phone digits, house number, full
   name or email; name+email only stays WARN/HOLD as today; historical figure ("Ada Lovelace, born 10 December 1815 in
   London") is not blocked. Extension test for the block card text. Run `python -m pytest -q -p no:cacheprovider` and
   the extension tests (`for f in extension/tests/*.test.js; do node --test "$f"; done`). Do not commit.
