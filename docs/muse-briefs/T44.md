# T44 — Contract-clause protection ([CONTRACT_CLAUSE_n])

Goal: when someone pastes text from a contract (NDA, MSA, SOW, employment or supplier agreement) into a chat with an AI,
the confidential clause text is replaced by a placeholder such as `[CONTRACT_CLAUSE_1]` before it leaves the machine,
and restored locally in the answer, exactly like the existing PII placeholders. Read `src/shadowfenced/engine.py`,
`redact.py`, `docclass.py` (the document classifier already labels contracts) and the placeholder tests first.

1. A Tier 1 detector `contract.py` that finds clause-shaped spans: numbered or headed clauses ("4.2 Termination",
   "Section 7 – Confidentiality", "Article III"), clauses with legal markers (shall, hereinafter, indemnify,
   notwithstanding, governing law, liquidated damages), and defined-party lines ("between Acme Ltd (the Supplier) and …").
   Each clause becomes one span labelled `CONTRACT_CLAUSE`; party names inside also get their normal PII/ORG handling.
2. Policy: action REDACT by default (placeholder, restored locally). If the document classifier says contract AND the
   text contains a confidentiality marker ("Confidential", "Do not distribute", "Privileged"), raise to HOLD with the
   reason "Confidential contract text" and offer the usual "Redact and send" choice.
3. A general legal question ("what does an indemnity clause usually mean?") with no pasted clause text must be ALLOW.
   Short quotes under about 12 words with no clause number are not redacted.
4. The receipt / risk card says "N contract clauses protected" and the descriptive trace explains which marker fired.
5. Tests with synthetic contracts only (invent company names): numbered NDA excerpt → [CONTRACT_CLAUSE_1..n] and
   restore round-trip; confidential MSA → HOLD; general legal question → ALLOW; clause + email → both placeholders;
   the proxy route `/v1/chat/completions` and `/api/send` also redact clauses. Run `python -m pytest -q`. Do not commit.
